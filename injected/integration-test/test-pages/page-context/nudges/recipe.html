Creamy Chicken & Mushroom Pie

By Sam Rivera · Prep 20 min · Cook 45 min · Serves 6 · ★ 4.8 (312 ratings)

This is the pie I make when the evenings draw in. Thigh meat stays juicy, the mushrooms bring depth, and a spoonful of Dijon keeps the creamy sauce from feeling heavy. Use shop-bought puff pastry — nobody will mind.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 800g boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
  • 30g butter
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 300g chestnut mushrooms, quartered
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 200ml double cream
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 small bunch tarragon, chopped
  • 320g ready-rolled puff pastry
  • 1 egg, beaten

Method

  1. Heat oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6.
  2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and brown the chicken in batches until golden. Set aside.
  3. Melt the butter in the same pan and cook the onion for 8 minutes until soft. Add the garlic and mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes more.
  4. Stir in the flour for 1 minute, then gradually add the stock, followed by the cream and mustard. Simmer for 5 minutes until thickened.
  5. Return the chicken and any juices to the pan, stir through the tarragon and season. Tip into a 1.5-litre pie dish.
  6. Lay the pastry over the top, trim and crimp the edges, cut a small steam hole and brush with beaten egg. Bake for 30–35 minutes until puffed and golden.

Tips

The filling can be made up to two days ahead and chilled; add the pastry just before baking. Leftover pie reheats well at 180C for 20 minutes, covered loosely with foil.
