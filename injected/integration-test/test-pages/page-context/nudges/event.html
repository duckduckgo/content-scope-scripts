About the show

After two sold-out nights at the Roundhouse, Lunar Static bring the Afterglow Tour back to London for one night only. Expect the full new record, a handful of deep cuts from Low Orbit, and the light show that has been the talk of the European leg.

Support comes from Bristol four-piece Paper Satellites, whose debut album was one of the year's quiet surprises.

Venue information

O2 Academy Brixton, 211 Stockwell Road, London SW9 9SL. Nearest station: Brixton (Victoria line), a 5-minute walk. The venue is standing only downstairs with limited seating in the circle. Bags larger than A4 are not permitted. Age 14+; under-16s must be accompanied by an adult.

Step-free access and an accessible viewing platform are available. Contact the venue's access team at least 48 hours before the show to book a space.
