Scientists map the deep ocean floor

A team of researchers has finished the most detailed map yet of a deep ocean trench in the Pacific.

The survey used autonomous submarines that spent six months collecting sonar data at depths of up to ten kilometres.

The map reveals underwater volcanoes, steep canyons and plains that had never been seen before.

The researchers say the data will help scientists understand earthquakes and the life that lives at great depth.
