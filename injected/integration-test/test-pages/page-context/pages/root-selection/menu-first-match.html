Hospital opens new children's wing

The regional hospital opened a new children's wing on Tuesday with forty beds and two operating theatres.

Every room has a fold-out bed so that a parent can stay overnight, and the playroom is open around the clock.

The wing was paid for by a public fundraising campaign that ran for six years and collected more than ten million.

Doctors said the new theatres would cut the waiting list for routine operations by about a third.

Nurses who moved from the old ward said the extra space would make night shifts much calmer for families.

Patients will move into the new wing over the next two weeks, starting with the youngest children.
