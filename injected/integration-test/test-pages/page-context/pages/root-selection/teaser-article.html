Court hears music advertising case

The pharmaceutical inspector ordered a rapper to stop advertising a medicine in a song that names the product.

The artist appealed the decision to the administrative court, arguing that the lyrics are a work of art and not an advertisement.

The ombudsman joined the case and warned that the ruling could have a chilling effect on artistic freedom of speech.

The inspector said that the song romanticised the use of medicines and could normalise risky behaviour among young listeners.

The court will hear both sides next month. A ruling is expected before the end of the year.
