City opens new public library

By Staff Reporter, updated this morning.

The new library opened its doors on Monday after three years of construction in the old market hall.

It holds more than two hundred thousand books, a music studio, a makerspace and a large reading room.

The mayor said the building would stay open until ten in the evening on weekdays and all day on Sunday.

Residents queued from early morning, and the first thousand visitors received a free library card bag.

The city plans to open two more branches in the northern districts within the next five years.
