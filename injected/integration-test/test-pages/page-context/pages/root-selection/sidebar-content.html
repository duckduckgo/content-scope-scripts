Bakery wins national bread award

A family bakery on the high street has won the national award for the best sourdough loaf of the year.

The owners use flour from a mill ten miles away and bake every loaf by hand before five in the morning.

The judges praised the crust, the open crumb and the slightly sour taste that comes from a slow rise.

They plan to open a second shop in the spring and to offer baking classes on Sunday afternoons.
