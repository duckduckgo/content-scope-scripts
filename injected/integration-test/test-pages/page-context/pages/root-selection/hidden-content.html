Ombudsman joins music advertising case

The ombudsman joined the court case about a song that names a medicine, citing freedom of speech.

The inspector had ordered the artist to stop what it called an unlawful advertisement for the product.

The court will hear both sides next month and is expected to rule before the end of the year.
