Bridge reopens after a year of repairs

The old stone bridge over the river reopened to traffic on Tuesday after twelve months of repair work.

Engineers replaced the cracked supports, resurfaced the road and added a wider path for bikes.

Buses will return to their usual route next week, and a speed limit of thirty applies on the bridge.

The council says the bridge should not need major work again for at least another fifty years.
